Welcome

The 3rd International Conference on Formal Methods and Foundations of Artificial Intelligence (FMF-AI 2027) will take place in Eger, Hungary, on 28–30 January 2027.

The conference brings together researchers working in artificial intelligence, formal methods and related areas of computer science. It provides a forum for presenting recent results, exchanging ideas and discussing theoretical foundations and practical applications.

Topics of Interest

  • Formal methods and verification
  • Mathematical and logical foundations of AI
  • Knowledge representation and reasoning
  • Machine learning and data science
  • Natural language processing
  • Intelligent and autonomous systems
  • Robotics and digital twins
  • Explainable and trustworthy AI
  • Applications of AI in education and industry
  • Theoretical computer science
