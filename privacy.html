Privacy Notice

Information on the processing of personal data in connection with registration for the 3rd International Conference on Formal Methods and Foundations of Artificial Intelligence (FMF-AI 2027), Eger, Hungary, 28–30 January 2027.

Draft for institutional review. This notice must be approved and updated to reflect the actual registration infrastructure and the confirmed responsibilities of the invoicing foundation before official registrations are accepted.

1. Data controller and contact

Eszterházy Károly Catholic University (Eszterházy Károly Katolikus Egyetem), 3300 Eger, Eszterházy tér 1., Hungary, is the proposed data controller for conference registration. The conference is organized by the University's Institute of Mathematics and Informatics (Matematikai és Informatikai Intézet).

For enquiries concerning personal data: adatvedelem@uni-eszterhazy.hu. University website: uni-eszterhazy.hu. The University's current designated data protection officer and contact arrangements should be confirmed before publication.

2. Purposes of processing and categories of data

The information submitted through the registration form is used for registering conference participants, administering participation and contributions, contacting participants, planning meals, arranging payment and invoicing, and responding to conference-related enquiries.

The form may collect name, e-mail address, institution, registration category, presentation and poster information (including titles), expected meal attendance, invoice/pro forma invoice requests, billing recipient, billing address, tax/VAT number, billing notes, optional dietary requirements and other comments. The registration system may also record the submission time and a technical request identifier.

Only information necessary for the relevant purpose should be provided. The dietary requirements field is optional. Please avoid including medical diagnoses or other sensitive personal information unless strictly necessary for the catering request.

3. Legal basis

Conference registration and the practical arrangements requested by participants are expected to rely on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where processing is necessary for an agreement with the participant or steps requested prior to it. Where applicable, limited administrative processing may rely on the organizer's legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, subject to the required assessment. Accounting and tax records are processed to comply with legal obligations under Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

Dietary information may reveal health or religious information, which can qualify as special-category data. Where such data are processed, an appropriate exception under Article 9 GDPR must be established; for example, explicit consent where applicable. The organizer must confirm and implement this safeguard before collecting such information in the final registration system.

4. Invoicing foundation and other recipients

Where an invoice or payment request is needed, the necessary billing and payment-related data will be provided to:

Abacus, az Informatikai és Matematikai Oktatásért és Kutatásért Közhasznú Alapítvány
3300 Eger, Leányka utca 4., Hungary
Tax number: 18586541-1-10

The Foundation will handle billing information for issuing invoices or pro forma invoices and fulfilling related legal duties. Its precise GDPR role (independent or joint controller, or processor, as applicable) and its privacy contact information must be confirmed in the institutional arrangements before this notice is finalized.

Personal data may be accessible, to the extent necessary, to authorized conference organizers, the University's IT personnel administering the registration system, and catering providers receiving only the attendance and dietary information they need. The organizers do not publish participants' billing or contact data as part of the public conference website.

5. Technical services and data location

The intended final arrangement is storage of registration data on infrastructure operated by Eszterházy Károly Catholic University, with the University's IT Directorate providing the technical hosting and backend.

Deployment verification required: the earlier test version of the system uses GitHub Pages for the public website, Google Apps Script / Google Sheets and e-mail services for registration handling, and Cloudflare Turnstile for automated-abuse prevention. If any of these services are still used when registration opens, the final notice must accurately identify their roles, relevant data transfers and appropriate contractual safeguards. The final text must reflect the actual system, not only the planned university-hosted arrangement.

6. Retention

Registration and organizational contact information will be retained only for the conference and the completion of related administrative follow-up, and then deleted or anonymized. A proposed maximum retention period is 12 months after the conference ends, subject to approval of the University's retention schedule and any need to resolve claims or disputes.

Invoices and other accounting documents are retained separately for the period required by applicable law. In Hungary, accounting vouchers must generally be preserved for at least eight years under Section 169(2) of Act C of 2000 on Accounting. Data necessary for those legal records are not deleted merely because the conference registration data have reached the end of their retention period.

7. Your rights

Subject to the conditions set by the GDPR, participants may request access to their personal data, correction, erasure or restriction of processing, and, where applicable, data portability or object to processing. Where processing is based on consent, it may be withdrawn at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

Requests can be submitted to adatvedelem@uni-eszterhazy.hu. Participants may also lodge a complaint with the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH), 1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9–11., Hungary; www.naih.hu, or seek a judicial remedy.

8. Information needed for registration

The fields marked as required in the registration form are necessary to handle registration. Without them, the organizers cannot complete the registration process. Meal choices and additional comments are optional. This registration process does not involve automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.
