Venue & Accommodation

Hotel Szent István

FMF-AI 2027 will be held at Hotel Szent István, Legányi Ferenc utca 12, 3300 Eger, Hungary.

Participants arrange accommodation directly with the hotel. Please use the booking code FMF-AI to receive the conference rate.

Accommodation Rates

RoomRate / person / night
Double or triple room18,000 HUF
Single room25,000 HUF
Local tourist tax750 HUF

Accommodation is not included in the conference fee. Breakfast and wellness access conditions are to be confirmed.
